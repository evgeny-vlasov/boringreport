Boring Report #01 — AI Significance Monitor

Robots Overlords

25 August–1 September 2026

States are moving AI into institutions—defence, regulation, procurement and sovereign compute.

Published

  1. Washington, DC: ChatGPT Mil launched on GenAI.mil.
  2. Brussels: ChatGPT was designated a Very Large Online Search Engine under the Digital Services Act.
  3. London: The £100m Sovereign AI R&D Procurement Scheme launched its first competitions.
  4. Kajaani: EuroHPC JU signed the LUMI-AI procurement contract.

Filtered / watchlist

  • Geneva: autonomous-weapons talks opened, but had no consequential outcome yet.
  • Basel: the FSB issued a warning and recommendation rather than an institutional change.
  • Beijing and Guangzhou: DiDi expanded driverless R2 trials, a real deployment that was insufficiently novel for this issue.
